Actual Luna data

Owner-authorized full postgame release of the completed luna1912-1018-r2 campaign. Private labels describe access during play, not restrictions after this release. Model-generated correspondence, reflections and memories are preserved. Raw API transcripts, complete prompts, invalid attempts and operational journals are not published. Original relative evidence locators/hashes identify retained raw artifacts; they are not links to publicly available raw files. Included normalized records supply the available evidence.
